# References and source notes

## Primary survey

Yang, X., Yu, E., and Lu, J. (2026). **Autonomous Drift Learning in Data Streams: A Unified Perspective.** arXiv:2605.01295.

- Abstract and HTML: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01295
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.01295
- Main contribution: a unified drift-learning taxonomy spanning time-stream, data-stream, and model-stream drift.
- Review corpus: 193 representative studies, with inclusion centered on peer-reviewed work from 2015–2025.

## Main companion paper for experiments and metrics

Cerqueira, V., Gomes, H. M., Heyden, M., Pfahringer, B., and Bifet, A. (2026). **A Framework for Evaluating and Benchmarking Concept Drift Detection Methods.** KDD 2026.

- Abstract and HTML: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07789
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07789
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3819070
- Reproducibility repository: https://github.com/vcerqueira/experiments-drift_evaluation
- Evaluation scope: 14 detectors, seven real datasets, four injected drift mechanisms, abrupt and gradual transitions, and 50 Monte Carlo trials per dataset/drift combination.
